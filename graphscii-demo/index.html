The mouse chooses edge crossings; the crossings choose GraphSCII bitmap ports. Side ports are the 16 exact 8×16 bitmap rows; top/bottom ports are the 8 exact bitmap columns. Every crossing is snapped once and mated exactly into the adjacent cell.

Slice 1 contract

The real pointer trajectory determines which cell edges are crossed. Each crossing snaps to the corresponding 3px GraphSCII bitmap row/column slot. The adjacent cell receives the exact mating L/R or T/B port; that crossing is never independently re-quantized.

Perpendicular corner ports are not collapsed. A corner traversal such as L15→B0 is a real GraphSCII semantic and emits its required one-pixel corner glyph. Multi-pass cells use an exact published connector glyph when that precise bitmap exists. Otherwise the already-solved straight GraphSCII glyphs are overstruck at the identical cell origin, preserving the exact union without moving ports or substituting a fill/approximation. Freehand, line, Bezier, and ellipse all use the same crossing compiler.

Debug Export records the exact pointer/coalesced-event path, every raw boundary crossing, every final GraphSCII segment, continuity checks, and the complete glyph panel so a bad stroke can be reproduced and inspected directly.

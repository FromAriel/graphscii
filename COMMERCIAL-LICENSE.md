# GraphSCII Commercial Licensing

**GraphSCII — Graphical Standard for Computer Information Interchange**  
Created and designed by **Ariel Williams**.

The public GraphSCII license is **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for noncommercial use**. Commercial rights are available separately.

## The short version

If you want to use GraphSCII commercially, **ask first**.

Businesses, publishers, studios, platforms, hardware vendors, SaaS products, paid applications, commercial games, commercial font bundles, advertising uses, and other projects whose use is primarily intended for commercial advantage or monetary compensation should obtain a separate written commercial license from Ariel Williams.

There is no fixed universal price. Commercial licensing is negotiated according to the project.

Factors may include:

- organization size;
- expected distribution;
- whether GraphSCII is a small supporting asset or a core product feature;
- whether the use is internal, customer-facing, embedded, sublicensed, or redistributed;
- revenue and funding level;
- whether modifications will be contributed back; and
- the scope and duration of the requested rights.

## What a commercial license can cover

A commercial agreement can grant some or all of the rights that the public noncommercial license does not grant for that use, including permission to use, bundle, embed, distribute, modify, or ship GraphSCII in a commercial product.

Depending on the agreement, Ariel Williams may also agree to different attribution or ShareAlike requirements for that specific commercial use.

Nothing is waived unless the written commercial permission says so.

## No automatic business license

This document explains the commercial licensing program. **It is not itself a commercial license.**

A commercial license exists only when Ariel Williams gives written permission identifying the permitted use or when a separate agreement is executed.

Credit alone does not create commercial permission. Neither does opening negotiations, receiving a quote, or using GraphSCII in a product that has not yet made money.

## Existing noncommercial rights remain

A separate commercial license does not cancel the public CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license for qualifying noncommercial uses. The two permission paths coexist.

## How to ask

Contact Ariel Williams using the public contact method listed with this project or Ariel Williams’s public GitHub profile and include:

1. your name or company;
2. the product/project name;
3. what GraphSCII will be used for;
4. how the product will be distributed;
5. whether users receive or can extract the font/data;
6. approximate company/team size and project scale; and
7. any deadline or launch date.

A brief request is fine. The goal is to match the license to the real use rather than force every commercial project into the same price.

## Indies

If you are a solo developer, tiny team, hobbyist, game-jam creator, small artist, or very early-stage project, see [`INDIE-LICENSE.md`](INDIE-LICENSE.md). The intended indie policy is much more informal and will often be free or low-cost.
